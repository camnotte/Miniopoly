# Incoming trade notifications

When another player sends you an offer or counteroffer, the game shows a small
**New trade offer** notice naming the sender, a numbered badge, and a short chime.
The notice has **Review offer** and dismiss controls. It never accepts a trade
or opens a dialog automatically. Review opens the incoming offer for inspection.

The number counts **pending incoming offers**, including offers you have viewed
but have not accepted or declined. It appears on Trading Center in the left
panel or phone toolbar, on Trading Center inside Game Menu, on the Properties &
trading category, and beside Game Menu itself. Counts update when offers are
accepted, declined, withdrawn, countered or expire. Outgoing offers do not notify
the sender, and other players do not receive alerts for your private negotiation.

The sound follows this browser's Sound and Volume settings; Reduced Motion does
not mute it. No operating-system notification permission is requested. Existing
pending offers remain badged after refresh/reconnect without repeated chimes.
If an offer arrives in a hidden tab, its alert can play once when the tab returns;
a withdrawn offer cannot produce a delayed chime. Muted/zero-volume arrivals do
not later play just because sound is enabled again. Browsers may require an
initial interaction before allowing audio; the badge and notice remain usable.
Closely arriving offers share a chime rather than layering sounds.

The notice dismisses after twelve visible seconds, while the badge remains until
the offers are resolved. Dismiss or open Trading Center to clear the current
notice. Game Menu hides the floating notice so it cannot cover menu options.
All trade terms, recipient consent, turn restrictions, holdings validation and
settlement remain controlled by the host.
