# Tsunami: proposals for a more active survival phase

These are discussion options, not active game rules in v0.3.95. The GO-payment correction and visual changes are implemented separately.

The current phase removes deeds and rent opportunities while construction remains the main meaningful action. More upgrades on a property whose protection is already spent do not buy another shield. This makes later turns feel less useful, and cash needs more tactical uses. Flooded squares should offer decisions without restoring ownership, rent or unlimited survival.

## Suggested additions

1. **Salvage sites on submerged properties.** Choose $75 cash or one construction material when landing. Limit collection to once per player per wave, across all salvage sites. The property stays submerged and produces no rent. Materials can fund emergency actions, not recreate lost deeds.
2. **Move a protection crew.** Before impact, pay $100 or use a material to move one unused protection from a safe property to a threatened one. The donor becomes vulnerable. This redistributes existing protection rather than endlessly creating shields. It needs a separate rule for transferring and consuming protection, not a change to the existing upgrade level alone.
3. **One emergency barrier per player per match.** Buy a $250 backup barrier and assign it before the announced wave. It blocks one hit, then disappears. No buying a replacement; no restoring a submerged property. This gives GO cash a purpose without making wealthy players permanently safe.
4. **Ride the current.** From a submerged square, pay $50 to choose a destination one to three spaces ahead once per wave. Resolve its rent or event normally. Emergency travel earns no extra GO reward. This lets players weigh an expensive Estate landing against a safer route.
5. **Rescue missions.** A flooded landing can offer a marked rescue job instead of salvage: spend cash or a material now for an emergency item. Reveal the cost and reward before the decision, and allow only one saved item. The advisor states the choice in one sentence.
6. **Distinct, announced wave types.** For example: a normal spread, a surge across neighboring properties, or a wave concentrated in one world. Show exact targets and the wave effect for one full round before impact. Variation should create preparation choices, not surprise elimination with no response.
7. **A small shared emergency market.** Offer one limited crew, material or route pass each wave. Players can buy or trade for it. Keep the choice brief, with no mandatory auction or long countdown on every turn.
8. **A stronger finale.** When two players remain, emphasize the next targets, remaining protected deeds and approaching impact; shorten repeated animations and build the storm sound. Retain the existing survival winner rules unless a separate finale rule is approved.

## Recommended prototype

Start with **salvage, protection transfer and announced wave variety**. These introduce an earning choice, a defensive tradeoff and changing threats. Allow one emergency action per turn so the mode remains quick; trading can remain available through the existing Trading Center.

Keep these limits: no resurrection of submerged deeds, no restored rent on flooded tiles, no unlimited shield purchases, and no new penalty on every flooded landing. A flooded square becomes an opportunity instead of an automatic reward or another punishment. Decide exact costs, caps and ties before implementation; the numbers above are starting points for playtesting.
