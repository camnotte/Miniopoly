MINIOPOLY v0.3.110 - FESTIVAL PAYOUTS AND ALL DEBT FUNDING

1. Fully Exit Game before replacing files in the COMPLETE normal game folder.
2. Extract the Update ZIP there and replace files. Keep saved games.
3. Restart the host and refresh EVERY player. Confirm v0.3.110.

The Festival visitor receives 80%, 70% or 60% with 2, 3 or 4 remaining players.
Other players split the remaining pool evenly. Whole-dollar rounding conserves
all money. Players in Setback and other neighborhoods still share.

All debt payments, INCLUDING RENT, enter the Festival fund. Owners no longer
receive rent cash. Taxes, card fees, Setback fees, ember damage and remaining
bankruptcy cash also enter the fund. Prior payments are not changed retroactively.

Improvements still count toward net worth at half the standard development cost
per retained valued stage, plus the deed price. Tsunami surcharges add no value.

Single Neighborhood Battle is a proposal for discussion, not a selectable mode
in this release. See SINGLE-NEIGHBORHOOD-BATTLE-DRAFT.md.

Use the Complete ZIP for a new installation. No match reset or pull request is needed.
Do not apply to the standalone High Ground prototype.
See UPDATE-v0.3.110.md.
