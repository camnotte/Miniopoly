# Countdown is now Tsunami

As of v0.3.78, the mode is named **Tsunami**. Its building rounds, property-survival
rules, one-use upgrade protection and winner selection are unchanged. Water,
submerged squares and dotted reference outlines replace cracks and void portals.

See [Tsunami rules and presentation](TSUNAMI.md) and [Trading Center](TRADING-CENTER.md).
Existing Countdown rooms and records open as Tsunami without resetting them.
