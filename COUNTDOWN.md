# Express · Countdown — first playable rules (v0.3.69)

Choose Countdown in Create room > Game Options, Host Game Setup or the launcher's new-game dialog. Choose 10, 15 or 20 building rounds. Classic and the existing World Collapse remain separate. Start a new Countdown match; existing matches are not converted.

Each player gets two distinct homes worth $400 total and one Building Permit without spending starting cash. Passing GO awards an available home alongside the normal cash reward. Another permit arrives halfway through building. **Building Permits keep their existing exception:** one immediate free upgrade without completing a set. Further paid upgrades need the complete unmortgaged color set.

During the final building round, mark one owned property somewhere across the three worlds. After everyone finishes the round, strike that property. Reveal two targets for the next full round, then three, four, five… targets. This rate is global, not per player or world. Only available, currently owned deeds are targeted; special spaces are never properties. Draw without replacement within a wave. If fewer eligible deeds remain, target all of them.

Every improved property has one automatic upgrade shield. On its first hit, it loses one upgrade and spends the shield, retaining the deed and any other upgrades. A later hit consumes the deed and every remaining upgrade, removes ownership and reduces net worth. An unupgraded property is consumed on its first hit. Shield history belongs to the property for the entire match: trading, selling or re-upgrading cannot reset it. Breaking a set does not remove a shield already earned. Building Permit upgrades qualify normally.

A consumed space collects no rent, cannot be purchased and has no property value. Landing or traveling across it is safe, including Gravity Pull. Players may buy, trade and upgrade remaining properties using ordinary rules. No separate cash shield shop and no movement evade tokens apply in Countdown.

After a consumption wave, players with no properties are eliminated. The last survivor wins. If a wave eliminates all remaining players, rank that group by properties held immediately before the wave; equal holdings share their place/victory. Earlier eliminations rank behind later ones. Cash remains in the statistics but does not override Countdown survival order. This shared-final-wave rule is an initial playtest choice.

The all-player status strip shows remaining property and available shield counts, including on phones. Threatened tiles show NEXT WAVE / SHIELDED; consumed tiles show CONSUMED · SAFE. Completed records use their own Countdown filter and retain shared places; Classic Legendary tables are separate.

## Multiplayer trading

Trading Center is available in all three modes. Send properties and/or cash, receive incoming-offer counts, accept, counter, decline or withdraw. A set-completion preview evaluates the complete proposed swap. Proposals and acceptance wait for movement and mandatory landing/purchase/payment decisions to finish. Recipients can respond off-turn at these safe points. Proposing constitutes the sender's consent; only the named recipient can accept. Accepted exchanges transfer all cash and deeds together on the host.

Property levels, mortgage state and Countdown shield history are retained. Ownership, levels, shield state, match identity and both cash balances are rechecked at acceptance. Changed or consumed assets invalidate old offers. Proposals are visible only to their participants. Offers persist across host restarts; new/reset matches clear them. Trades never cancel existing rent or debts. Rent negotiations and auctions remain separate future features.
