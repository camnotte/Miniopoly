# v0.3.45 — The Dog becomes a beagle

Dog now has full-body cartoon artwork: chestnut floppy ears, a dark saddle,
cream muzzle and paws, a teal collar and a gold tag. Four legs take alternating
steps, the tail wags and the body bobs while moving. The piece rests while parked,
during the die spin, in Setback and while being pulled by Gravity Pull.

It faces left/right and tilts 45 degrees up/down, following the board direction.
OS and in-game Reduced Motion stop its limb/tail/bounce effects. The same vector
works at desktop and phone sizes. The standalone game's picker and dog Setback
view also use it. Car/cat artwork, the five remaining emoji pieces, custom avatar
preferences, special-place GIFs, rules, balances and hosting settings are retained.

## Install

1. Stop hosting. Extract Miniopoly-v0.3.45-Update.zip into your existing installed
   v0.3.40, v0.3.41, v0.3.42, v0.3.43 or v0.3.44 folder and replace matching files.
2. Restart Start-Miniopoly.bat and refresh every player's browser. Confirm
   v0.3.45 beside Room. Existing matches and saved player seats need no reset.
3. The update includes the board HTML; replace it as well as the shared scripts.
   This ZIP is larger than the avatar/cash fixes because the board embeds its art.

A fresh/incomplete installation needs the complete Windows ZIP or ALL FOUR v0345
numbered downloads, extracted together. Node.js LTS is required. No older game
ZIP is required by the complete package.

## View and test

The current multiplayer assignment is Car, Cat, Dog, Tablet. To see Dog in a
live match, use three players; Player 3 receives Dog. This update does not add a
piece-selection screen to multiplayer. The separate Dog Preview HTML works
offline, with direction and Reduced Motion controls and full-size/board-size art.

Check that the dog appears at GO before rolling, rests during the cube animation,
walks with a wagging tail, faces each corner correctly and returns to a still
piece on arrival. Repeat on a phone, during a world shift/Gravity Pull and with
Reduced Motion enabled. The parked token must never appear early at the
server's destination. Actual player positions and landing finances retain their
existing timing and values. A chat update must not restart movement.
