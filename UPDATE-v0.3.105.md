# Miniopoly v0.3.105 — Detailed property inspection, railroad improvements and status fixes

Click a board property to see a compact rent table covering every House/Chalet/Estate stage, the owner of each member of its color set across neighborhoods, and its current flood-protection status. Mortgage/Setback pauses are identified. The current rent uses the same development multipliers as the host; an old property-popup formula that understated Estate rent has been replaced. Submerged sites can also be inspected. Popups fit the visible portion of the embedded board, with their Close control kept reachable.

Railroads can be developed independently through Depot, Terminal and Grand Station, at $100 per improvement or $200 in Tsunami. No color/railroad set is required; Building Permits can pay for an improvement. The landed railroad earns 1.5×, 2× or 3× its ordinary ownership-count rent, rounded up. Improvements appear in its three board slots, paid/free development choices, rent previews, trade estimates and upgraded-property overview. Each funded improvement adds $50 to net worth/resale. Mortgage, turn, cash, cap and ownership checks are enforced by the host. A developed railroad gains the same one-use flood shield as a home: one hit removes one improvement, then the shield stays spent. Utilities remain undevelopable.

The wave counter now shows the complete WAVE N and of 5 on separate lines so the total is not clipped. Property Status labels the personal section Your properties, and replaces This neighborhood with All neighborhoods, using totals across all players and all three neighborhoods. Submersion, spent shields and temporary Wave Shields are counted consistently; combined protection never counts a property twice. Changing the displayed board does not change these global totals.

Last auction results now keeps its expanded/collapsed state and scroll position through routine snapshots, actions and live updates. A new auction's results begin collapsed. Bids, payment, wave timing and final scoring retain their existing rules.

Fully Exit Game, install into the complete normal game folder, restart the launcher/server, refresh all players and confirm v0.3.105. Existing directional matches can continue without a reset. Read TSUNAMI-RULES.md for railroad prices, rent, protection and valuation.
