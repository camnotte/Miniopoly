NEXT WAVE FORECAST

How to survive

Choose one refuge. Each turn: roll, then use up to one construction action. You may construct before rolling instead. All players finish their turn before the next round or wave.

Raise increases height. Homes must sit strictly above the forecast water. Reinforce increases support capacity by one, up to four. Repair restores all support strength. Waves remove the announced impact from supports—even above water.

A Building Permit pays for one construction action. It does not grant an extra action. Each supporting property pays $60 between rounds. Passing GO pays $100. Other special squares grant $40. Rent is limited to available cash; this prototype has no bankruptcy debt.

One supporting property may submerge each wave. A failed refuge gets one evacuation if another owned property remains. Salvaged construction loses one height level; supports restart at two. Evacuation never transfers another player’s property.

Last survivor wins. After five waves, compare height, then remaining supports. Exact ties share victory. If everyone is eliminated, the latest eliminated players are compared by height and supports.
