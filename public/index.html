Game rules

Play until one player remains, or agree on a round limit before starting.

Roll the 12-sided cube. A 6 or 12 shifts your world before moving; ten rolls without a shift also move you to another world.

Individual shifts move only the roller.

A 7 or 11 earns another roll after the landing is resolved. Three in a row send you to Setback.

Leave Setback by rolling 6 or 12 for free, choosing a paid roll costing 20× its result, forfeiting an eligible deed, or using an escape card. After three failed free attempts, the locked roll requires payment. Escape rolls never shift worlds or earn a bonus. You cannot collect rent while held.

Community Festival replaces Community Bonus in The Communities. Paid Town Tax and Storm Fund charges build one shared fund. The visitor receives 60%; the remaining 40% is divided among other nonbankrupt players across every world, including Setback. Whole dollars are paid in turn order, and the fund resets to zero.

Complete property sets to unlock upgrades, or use a Building Permit for one free upgrade without a complete set. Net worth includes cash, unmortgaged property prices and half of upgrade costs.
