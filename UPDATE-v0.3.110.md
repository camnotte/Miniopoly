# Miniopoly v0.3.110 — Festival payouts and all debt funding

The player landing on Community Festival receives 80% of the fund with two remaining players, 70% with three, and 60% with four. The remaining 20%, 30% or 40% is split evenly among the other nonbankrupt players, including players in Setback or another neighborhood. The calculation uses remaining eligible players rather than room capacity. All dollars are paid once, with whole-dollar remainders distributed in turn order. Each payout records its actual percentage so saved/reconnected celebrations retain their original split; old already-paid Festivals retain their prior 60/40 presentation and are not recalculated.

As explicitly requested, all cash debt payments now go into the shared Festival fund, including rent, taxes, negative card fees, paid/forced Setback escape, ember damage and remaining cash paid in bankruptcy. Property owners no longer receive rent cash directly. Rent amounts and development multipliers still apply. Bankruptcy deed-collateral rules remain unchanged; remaining cash goes to the fund. Purchases, paid development, rebuilding, trades and auction bids retain their existing investment/transfer rules. Waived fees and unsuccessful payments do not add money, and repeated Pay requests cannot add it twice.

Payment dialogs identify the Festival as the destination and show the property owner separately. Festival celebrations, board descriptions, help text and launcher rules show the new splits and funding sources. New funds/debits are checked before cash is changed; overflow cannot partially charge a debt. The live-auction cash-reservation and required-payment rules continue to apply.

Improvements already count in net worth: cash + functioning deed price + half the standard improvement cost per retained valued stage. Copper Lane at three Estates (six development stages) contributes $60 + 6 × $25 = $210, in addition to the player's cash. Tsunami construction surcharges do not add value; submerged sites contribute zero until rebuilt. This valuation is unchanged.

Single Neighborhood Battle is a design proposal in SINGLE-NEIGHBORHOOD-BATTLE-DRAFT.md, not an implemented mode in this release. It outlines one neighborhood, immediate development without sets, a short round limit and possible unique upgrades for review.
