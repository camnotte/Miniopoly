# Miniopoly v0.3.32 — Confirm your avatar

Create Room and Join Room now show a review card immediately below the Men and Women avatar rows. It displays the currently selected cartoon portrait or personal photo with a **Confirm avatar** button. After confirmation, the card reads **Avatar confirmed** and enables the form's Create/Join button.

Changing your selection or replacing/removing the selected photo requires confirmation again. Scrolling through portraits does not change your selection or confirmation. A failed photo upload keeps your previous choice. Confirmation is separate for Create and Join, and resets on refresh or return from a room; your saved avatar/photo preference remains available. Existing room seats resume normally.

The bonus-roll summary and its Setback explanation are removed from the Create form. The gameplay rules remain unchanged and can still be read in Game Menu.

## Install

Stop the game server. Extract **Miniopoly-v0.3.32-Update.zip** and copy all contents into your existing game folder, replacing matching files. Run **Start-Miniopoly.bat**, choose your usual hosting mode, and refresh all player browsers. Confirm **v0.3.32** beside Room. Existing saved rooms, hosting settings, certificates and launchers are retained.

## Review

Choose a portrait, check the review card, and confirm it. The Create/Join button should enable. Change the portrait and check that confirmation is required again. Upload a photo and confirm its preview before submitting. Repeat on a phone and Join Room. Scrolling through either avatar row should preserve confirmation until a different portrait is selected.
