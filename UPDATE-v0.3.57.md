# v0.3.57 — Championship ceremony and Community Festival

Stop hosting and choose **Exit Launcher** from the Windows system tray. Extract
the Update ZIP into the **existing v0.3.56 folder**, replacing matching files.
Keep the public, lib, hosting and scripts folders in place. Reopen
**Start-Miniopoly.bat**, start hosting, and refresh Windows and guest browsers.
Confirm **v0.3.57** beside Room. The complete package also works without an older
installation; Node.js LTS is required. Existing saves and hosting settings remain.

The championship has an illustrated three-world backdrop, a gold trophy,
large player portraits and selected pieces, gold/silver/bronze podiums, actual
final balances and asset counts, and honest Legendary record recognition.
Two-player games have two podium places. Co-champions receive equal gold podiums
and shared rank numbers. Four players remain visible in the final standings.

A three-second entrance adds sweeping lights, confetti, a number count-up and
an optional synthesized fanfare. **Skip entrance** ends it immediately. Reduced
Motion displays the complete results without the animation or sound; normal
sound and volume preferences apply. No player is automatically returned to setup.
**View final board**, **Replay ceremony**, **Hall of Fame**, and the host's eligible
ranked rematch remain available. Chat stays above the results.

**Save victory image** downloads a local PNG containing the match name, date,
winners, portraits and final standings. A personal avatar is included if used;
the image is not uploaded or posted anywhere. Each player independently selects
**Return to Create / Join** when ready. Scoring, ranking ties and record rules are
unchanged. Upgrade counts describe actual owned upgrade levels.

Community Festival now has detailed gazebo/village-green artwork. When the piece
arrives, a large banner and finite fireworks appear over the center of the board.
The following payout window shows a huge **Community Festival** ribbon, fund
total, portraits, and the exact award to every eligible player. Empty festivals
show $0 honestly. The 60% visitor / 40% shared rule, tax funding, Setback eligibility
and once-only server payment are unchanged. Reduced Motion shows a still banner
and payouts. Reopening or refreshing a result never distributes the fund again.

Also includes the enlarged tennis shoes and launcher-startup restore hotfix.

For a review without ending a game, open **http://localhost:3000/championship-preview**
while hosting locally. It uses clearly labeled sample results, never changes a
room, and offers two/three/four players and shared-winner examples. The standalone
HTML preview runs independently of the game server and embeds its font/artwork.
