# Single Neighborhood Battle — design proposal

This is a proposal for discussion. v0.3.110 updates the Festival; it does not add this mode to Create room yet.

The host chooses Richmond Hill, The Communities or Coastal Georgia. All players stay on that one board for the match. Rolling 6 or 12 moves normally within the same neighborhood. Color sets are not required for development.

Suggested first prototype:

- Two to four players, $1,000 starting cash, eight rounds by default, with ten as an option.
- Buy and make one improvement on the same turn. Limit each player to one development per turn, including a free Building Permit development, so cash cannot buy all six stages at once.
- Reuse the current House/Chalet/Estate artwork initially. Tune costs and rents for this small board before choosing final unique upgrade names or powers.
- Include an accessible Community Festival square on every selectable board, preserving GO, Setback and the card spaces. Richmond Hill and Coastal Georgia could replace their tax square with a Festival; The Communities already has one.
- Apply the new shared-fund rules: rent and bank debts enter the pot, and the visitor receives 80/70/60% with 2/3/4 remaining players. Landlords retain their deeds and improvement value but do not receive rent directly.
- In the last two rounds, announce a Final Showdown with a moderate rent increase. Keep the round limit short; a sole survivor wins early, otherwise use final net worth.

Possible unique development choices for a later prototype:

| Path | Benefit | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landmark | Higher rent charged to visiting opponents and paid into the Festival | Drain opponents' cash and grow the jackpot. |
| Safehouse | A limited discount on the owner's next required payment | Preserve cash before a risky lap. |
| Commerce | A modest owner bonus for passing GO | Offer reliable income alongside the shared jackpot. |

A board full of high rents can make the Festival landing dominate the result, because all rent goes into the communal pot. A short round limit and restrained early rent increases are sensible starting points. Playtest before adding all three paths, extra cards or hostile property takeovers.

Current valuation remains cash + functioning deed price + half the standard cost of retained valued development stages. Spending on an upgrade can lower immediate net worth because its resale value is less than its cost. Full-cost valuation of improvements is an alternative worth testing specifically for Battle if rewarding construction more strongly is the aim; it is not part of this update.
