# Miniopoly v0.3.29 — Cartoon player avatars

Ten portraits replace the old emoji avatars: five men and five women, approximately ages 30–60, with distinctive face shapes, hairstyles and colors. These are the latest approved designs.

Choose an avatar on Create Room or Join Room. The browser remembers your preference, and the room saves your chosen portrait so every player sees the same face in the lobby, Players & Standings and board ownership/world views. Existing saved rooms automatically show one of the new portraits. Older standalone emoji choices map to a new portrait.

The moving game pieces, car/cat artwork, size and movement effects are unchanged.

## Install on your Windows host

1. Stop the running game server.
2. Extract **Miniopoly-v0.3.29-Update.zip** and copy all its contents into your existing Miniopoly game folder, replacing files when asked.
3. Run **Start-Miniopoly.bat** and choose Local Multiplayer or Online Players as usual. The existing local and online launchers also remain available.
4. Refresh every player's browser. Confirm **v0.3.29** beside Room. If necessary use Ctrl+F5 on Windows or Command+Shift+R on Mac.

This update preserves your existing room saves, hosting settings and certificates.

## Review

Create a room with one avatar, then join from another browser/device with a different avatar. Check both faces in the lobby and Players & Standings after starting. Refresh either browser and confirm the choices remain. Roll to check that the original game pieces still move as before. Both Create and Join offer all ten portraits, including on phones. Existing rooms receive replacement faces automatically; choose your preferred face when joining a new room.
