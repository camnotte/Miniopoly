MINIOPOLY v0.3.100 - NEIGHBORHOOD CENTER, ADVISOR STATUS AND FESTIVAL FUND

1. Fully Exit Game from the launcher.
2. Extract the Update ZIP into your COMPLETE normal Miniopoly folder.
3. Replace existing files. Keep saved-game files.
4. Relaunch and refresh every player. Confirm v0.3.100.

The center shows a larger neighborhood name and the active player's turn.
The graphic die, last roll, Community Chest and Chance decks occupy the
bottom area. Disaster mode, scenario and wave/building progress have their
own upper-left panel.

Submerged and protected counts are in the advisor window. They describe
ALL players' properties in the neighborhood being viewed, including board
previews. They remain visible with hints off or the advisor minimized.

The shared Festival fund is displayed on the actual Community Festival
square in The Communities. It is not shown on Community Chest squares or
in the center. The payout rules, property art/values and saves are unchanged.

See UPDATE-v0.3.100.md. For a new installation use the Complete ZIP.
Do not apply to the small standalone High Ground prototype.
