# Miniopoly v0.3.102 — Property Status, compact landings and trade filters

1. The advisor window is named Property Status.
2. The live die and turn-label group sit slightly lower, with the die's permanent frame and fill removed. A keyboard-only focus indicator remains.
3. The actual 6/12 transition banner and movement notifications now say NEIGHBORHOOD SHIFT. Human-readable menu descriptions and transition badges are corrected without changing internal message or save keys.
4. Personal all-neighborhood totals now appear separately from the viewed-neighborhood counts. Developed properties, currently Protected properties and spent shields are distinguished. The earlier local counter could correctly show one when the viewer developed properties in two neighborhoods. A shield used by a previous wave can also leave a property developed but unprotected. The one-use rule is preserved.
5. Landing dialogs have a compact content area and a fixed action/footer area. The artwork, margins and financial rows use less height. Long optional content can scroll while decision buttons stay visible. Property Status remains outside the landing dialog. The real property and Community Chest landing flows were checked at laptop and phone viewport heights.
6. Trading Center adds a Show properties filter below List properties by: All properties / Needed for your sets. Missing residential members of partially owned color sets are identified across neighborhoods and opponents, with clickable matching-opponent shortcuts. The give list remains available for negotiating. Selected incoming rows remain visible when a filter changes, so no selected offer is hidden. Best value first ranks estimated collectible rent against original deed price; utilities use an average 6.5 die, mortgages earn zero, and the comparison is not a negotiated price or guarantee. Existing color and price ordering remain available.

No server trade authorization or settlement rules change. No Tsunami wave mechanics change. See [TSUNAMI-RULES.md](TSUNAMI-RULES.md) for the current 1, 2, 3… global wave sequence, forecasts, one-use protection, safe submerged landings and victory rules.
