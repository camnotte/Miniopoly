# Miniopoly v0.3.106 — Auction winner announcements, personal helper and rolling tsunami

Every completed blind auction now opens a results screen for each active player. It lists every site, its winner and winning price, including ties and bank-held sites with no positive bid. Each winner receives their own paid total, available cash, rebuilding costs and clear next steps: wait for your turn if necessary, open Storm preparations → Rebuild auction sites, then pay to restore base rent and deed value. Show rebuilding options opens the existing validated controls; Got it or Close acknowledges the receipt. Acknowledgments persist per room, match and player in that browser, so routine refresh/reconnect does not repeat them. Up to five resolved auctions are retained by the host; unread receipts can be presented after reconnect. Live bids remain private until closing. Muting optional helper tips does not suppress winner announcements.

A personal helper bubble appears at the bottom left, with the player's chosen avatar, one short useful message, an optional action, Got it, Close and Mute helper. It explains rebuilding, wave warnings, trades, payments, useful developments and the final recovery round. Reading/dismissing a tip keeps that same tip dismissed through refresh/reload. No persistent floating launcher remains over the board after dismissal. Helper hints are on by default, remembered per browser, and can be turned off or restored in Game Options → Player helper hints. Tips hide while dialogs, movement or wave effects are active, and do not take keyboard focus.

A new illustrated curling crest rolls across the board from the announced direction over eight seconds. The effect is confined to the board; side controls remain visible. Auction entry waits until the sweep finishes, with the original host deadline still in effect. Only the properties actually destroyed in that wave break into fragments and dust as the crest reaches them. Surviving protected properties flash as they absorb the hit. Destroyed properties become brown rubble and dirt with a small turquoise puddle; their original intact-property picture is covered. Their name and auction/rebuild status remain readable. Property and landing popups also show the ruined-site art until the site is rebuilt. Rebuilding restores the original property artwork. Reduced motion skips demolition and uses a short transition; reconnecting does not replay an old wave.

These are presentation and guidance changes. Bid validation, winners, prices, cash, rebuilding, protection, wave targets and final scoring remain host-authoritative. Existing directional matches continue without a reset.

Fully Exit Game, install into the complete normal game folder, restart the launcher/server, refresh all players and confirm v0.3.106.
