# Miniopoly v0.3.30 — Avatar browsing and personal photos

The latest approved five men and five women replace the previous portraits, retaining their different head angles, eye directions and expressions. Moving game pieces and their effects are unchanged.

## Choose your avatar

On Create Room or Join Room, use the left/right arrow buttons to browse the portraits, then click or tap a portrait to select it. You can also swipe the row on a phone or focus a portrait and use the keyboard arrow keys. Browsing does not change your selection until you select a portrait. The selection is remembered in this browser when storage is available.

**Upload photo** accepts PNG, JPG and WebP files up to 8 MB. The browser center-crops the image to a square, resizes it to a small thumbnail and discards original photo metadata. Your photo appears as an additional choice at the end of the row. No photo is sent until you create or join a room. It then becomes visible to the room's players in the lobby, standings and board avatar displays, and stays with your seat after a refresh or host restart.

Select a cartoon portrait to switch back while keeping your uploaded photo available. **Remove photo** clears the saved photo in this browser. Existing room seats retain the portrait they joined with; choose a new avatar when creating or joining a new room. Photo removal in the picker does not alter an already saved room seat.

## Update your Windows installation

1. Stop the running game server.
2. Extract **Miniopoly-v0.3.30-Update.zip** and copy all contents into your existing game folder, replacing matching files.
3. Run **Start-Miniopoly.bat** and select Local Multiplayer or Online Players as usual. All existing launchers remain included.
4. Refresh every player's browser and confirm **v0.3.30** beside Room. Use Ctrl+F5 on Windows or Command+Shift+R on Mac if needed.

Existing saved rooms, hosting settings and certificates are retained.

## Quick review

Create a room using an uploaded photo. Join from another device with a cartoon avatar. Check that both devices show the same two portraits, start the game, then refresh. Check the arrows and swipe browsing on a phone, and roll to confirm the original car and cat still move as before.
