WHAT TO REVIEW

Card-wave effects.

This preview uses sample positions. Your game and saves are unchanged.

  1. 1
    Broader ocean swells

    Slower, irregular swells replace the repeated diagonal streaks.

  2. 2
    Readable submerged properties

    Shallow wash moves over the artwork; names, dotted boundaries and NO RENT stay clear.

  3. 3
    Centered battleship and narrower wake

    The ship crosses the squares again. Original names and prices stay readable above it; bow foam streams backward.

  4. 4
    Stone seawalls

    Low masonry protects homes without covering their buildings with a bubble.

  5. 5
    Waves washing onto the decks

    A small swell curls against each card, runs up its lower edge with foam, then drains away. Lettering stays clear.

  6. 6
    Correct tile information

    GO, tax, rail, utility and property tiles use captures from the actual game renderer.

  7. 7
    Prominent wave status

    TSUNAMI · WAVE 2 is larger above the world name; submerged-property totals stay secondary.

Same Tsunami rules. Water is a visual treatment. Submerged spaces stay safe, collect no rent and have no property value. Seawalls still protect once.
