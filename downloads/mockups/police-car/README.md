# Police car — design mockup

A design review only. The Miniopoly game is unchanged.

The concept uses the current car's rounded silhouette and body/window geometry,
repainted in black and ivory with a small gold shield and POLICE door lettering.

- **Police-Car-Motion.gif**: looping preview of visible wheel rotation, suspension
  bounce, soft alternating red/blue roof lights, tracks and a parked spotlight.
- **Police-Car-Motion.mp4**: the same preview as a small video.
- **Police-Car-Design.png**: full design sheet with moving and parked poses.
- **Police-Car-Arrival.png**: arrival with bright headlights and a spotlight sweep.
- **Police-Car-Mockup.html**: interactive standalone mockup. On GitHub, choose
  Download raw file, then open the downloaded HTML in a browser. All artwork and
  fonts are embedded; it does not connect to a game server.
- **Police-Car.svg**: parked vector concept.

The wheels have cream spokes and an asymmetric gold marker to show rotation.
The car leaves twin fading tread rows and its antenna flexes with the suspension.
The roof lights alternate gently and cast a subtle reflection on the road.
At arrival the wheels stop, the headlights brighten and a small spotlight sweeps
forward. The interactive version includes Play / Pause, Replay, Show arrival,
direction, slow motion, and light and track toggles. Reduced Motion starts it
paused; Play explicitly starts motion.

Checked in Chromium: travel, wheels, body bounce, tracks, antenna flex, red/blue
alternation, parked wheels and spotlight motion, the spotlight staying off during
travel, direction and track/light toggles, Reduced Motion and Play, mobile width,
no script errors and no external asset requests.

No game piece, release version, rules or saves were changed. The current game's
source files, working branch and HEAD remain unchanged.
