MINIOPOLY v0.3.106 - AUCTION WINNERS, PERSONAL HELPER AND ROLLING TSUNAMI

1. Fully Exit Game from the launcher before replacing files.
2. Extract the Update ZIP into your COMPLETE normal Miniopoly folder.
3. Replace files. Keep saved games.
4. Restart the launcher/server and refresh every player.
5. Confirm v0.3.106. Existing directional matches can continue.

Completed auctions announce each winner and give personal rebuilding steps.
Got it dismisses the receipt; Show rebuilding options opens Storm preparations.
Only winning bidders pay. A damaged site still needs its separate rebuilding fee.

The helper appears at bottom left, on by default. Read a tip and choose Got it
or Close to remove it. Mute helper hides optional hints. Restore it in
Game Options -> Player helper hints. Auction winner notices remain visible.

A slower directional crest crosses the board before bidding appears. Destroyed
properties break apart and become rubble/dirt with a small puddle. Rebuilding
restores their original artwork. Reduced motion skips the demolition animation.

See UPDATE-v0.3.106.md and TSUNAMI-RULES.md.
Use the Complete ZIP for a new installation. No pull request is needed.
Do not apply to the small standalone High Ground prototype.
