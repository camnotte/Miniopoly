Why this is still a proposal

Base residential prices and rent are consistent. Estate jumps, set size, railroad network returns, upgrade valuation and minimum cleaning fees create larger differences. Candidate B matches developed returns more closely, but the modeled unlimited games still rarely end.

Candidate B uses a 1/2/3/4/7/11/16 residential rent curve, proportional construction, full funded construction value, and service prices scaled to opponents. The earlier proposal uses 1/2/3/4/8/12/18 with unchanged upgrade costs and 5%/8% service fees.
