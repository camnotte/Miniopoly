MINIOPOLY v0.3.66 - CHAMPION CELEBRATION AND PIECE SOUNDS

Stop hosting and fully Exit Launcher from the Windows system tray.
Extract the update into the game folder containing server.js, or extract the
complete package into a new folder. Start the launcher from the updated folder.
Refresh every browser and confirm v0.3.66.

The trophy has a gold 1 and CHAMPION title. The winning piece circles the cup
and plays its own sound. Other podium pieces sit to the right of their amounts.
All ten pieces have movement sounds; the Battleship leaves a foamy water wake.
Use Game Options for Sound, Volume and Reduced Motion. Click/tap once if your
browser needs an interaction before allowing sound.

Express / World Collapse and Classic remain. Existing saved games are preserved.
Police car and School bus remain included; Monster truck remains excluded.
Node.js 18+ is required; no npm install is needed.
See UPDATE-v0.3.66.md for changes, installation and checks.
