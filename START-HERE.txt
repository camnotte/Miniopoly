MINIOPOLY v0.3.70 - NEW GAME MENU

Stop hosting and fully Exit Launcher from the Windows system tray.
Apply the update over your complete game folder, or extract Complete into a new
folder. Relaunch, refresh every browser and confirm v0.3.70.
Existing rooms and seats can resume; no new match is required for this menu.

Open Game Menu (Menu on phones). Six categories expand to further options.
Desktop submenus open to the left; phone sections expand inline.
Sound and Hints: checkmark means ON, empty box means OFF.
Preferences contains Volume, Game font, and Board & panels.
Room & hosting contains match options, invitations, seat tools and recovery.

Countdown, trading, current World Collapse rules and saved preferences remain.
See GAME-MENU.md, UPDATE-v0.3.70.md and DOWNLOAD-v0.3.70.md.
