MINIOPOLY v0.3.77 - PIECE SOUND/EFFECTS AND TRADING LISTS

Stop hosting and fully Exit Launcher from the Windows system tray.
Apply the update over your complete game folder, or extract Complete into a new
folder. Relaunch, refresh all browsers and confirm v0.3.77.
Existing rooms and pending trades can resume.

Moving pieces are 30% bigger; landed size is unchanged.
New recorded effects, fictional bus/police radio chatter, stronger lights,
T-Rex tracks/teeth and front-only battleship spray are included.
Trading Center property lists sort By color or By price without clearing terms.
Round/building/wave status is above the ready-turn popup, not in the turn panel.

Open /sound-review on your game host to listen, or use the standalone review.
