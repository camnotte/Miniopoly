MINIOPOLY v0.3.102 - PROPERTY STATUS, COMPACT LANDINGS AND TRADE FILTERS

1. Fully Exit Game from the launcher before replacing files.
2. Extract the Update ZIP into your COMPLETE normal Miniopoly folder.
3. Replace existing files. Keep saved-game files.
4. Relaunch the launcher/server and refresh every player.
5. Confirm v0.3.102. Restarting serves the new trade-filter script.

Advisor is now Property Status. Your all-neighborhood protection and
construction totals are separate from the displayed neighborhood totals.
Spent shields are identified. The die sits slightly lower with no frame.
Shift announcements use Neighborhood instead of World.

Landing popups are compact, with the action buttons kept visible and any
long content scrolling separately. The Property Status panel remains
outside landing decisions.

Trading Center adds Needed for your sets and Best value first. Matching
opponents are listed. Value means estimated rent / original deed price,
not a negotiated trade price. Selected properties remain visible.

Read TSUNAMI-RULES.md for the current round and one-use protection rules.
See UPDATE-v0.3.102.md. For a new installation use the Complete ZIP.
Do not apply to the small standalone High Ground prototype.
