MINIOPOLY v0.3.75 - COMPACT, MOVABLE MATCH ACTIVITY

Stop hosting and fully Exit Launcher from the Windows system tray.
Apply the update over your complete game folder, or extract Complete into a new
folder. Relaunch, refresh all browsers and confirm v0.3.75.
Existing rooms and seats can resume.

Match Activity is now a compact floating window on the left.
Drag its header, or focus it and use arrow keys (Shift moves farther).
Use -/+ to minimize/restore and the reset arrow to return it to the left.
Its placement and minimized state are remembered in this browser.
Game Menu > Results & activity > Match activity restores and focuses it.

Chat remains independent. Moving portals and ember recovery rules are retained.
See MATCH-ACTIVITY.md, UPDATE-v0.3.75.md and DOWNLOAD-v0.3.75.md.
