MINIOPOLY v0.3.98 - PROPERTY OWNER AVATARS

1. Fully Exit Game from the launcher.
2. Extract the Update ZIP into your COMPLETE normal Miniopoly folder.
3. Replace existing files. Keep saved-game files.
4. Relaunch and refresh every player. Confirm v0.3.98.

Properties use the normal flat format. Circular player portraits
replace the owner-name capsules. Hover for the owner name, or tap
the portrait to open property details. Built-in and uploaded avatars
are supported on every neighborhood and in every game mode.

Stilts and angled platforms have been removed. Existing flood
protection uses a small footer shield indicator. Current game rules,
rents, developments and saved ownership are unchanged. The proposed
multi-disaster and insurance rules are not part of this update.

See UPDATE-v0.3.98.md. For a new installation use the Complete ZIP.
Do not apply to the small standalone High Ground prototype.
