MINIOPOLY v0.3.76 - INCOMING TRADE ALERTS

Stop hosting and fully Exit Launcher from the Windows system tray.
Apply the update over your complete game folder, or extract Complete into a new
folder. Relaunch, refresh all browsers and confirm v0.3.76.
Existing rooms and pending trades can resume.

Incoming trade offers and counteroffers show a notice naming the sender,
a numbered pending-offer badge and a short chime. Review opens the offer.
Badges appear on Trading Center and in Game Menu, including phone layouts.
Sound follows the recipient's Sound/Volume preferences. Refreshing does not
repeatedly chime for existing offers. The badge clears as offers are resolved.

See TRADE-NOTIFICATIONS.md, UPDATE-v0.3.76.md and DOWNLOAD-v0.3.76.md.
