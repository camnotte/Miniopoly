SWAP FAVOR: On your turn between moves, open Trading Center > Swap Favor,
or Turn Actions > Perks > Offer position swap. The other player must accept.
One sender token is used only on acceptance. Neither player can be in Setback.
Swapping triggers no GO reward or landing effect. You keep your cash and deeds.

NEGOTIATIONS: Choose Negotiate rent on a rent bill. Propose an amount; the
owner can accept, decline or counter. After agreement, CLOSE Trading Center
and choose PAY on the bill. Rent still pays 90% owner / 10% Festival.
Bank fees, taxes and Setback payments cannot be negotiated.
Offers use the Trading Center inbox with unread badges and sound alerts.

Existing matches and saved Swap Favor cards continue. No reset or PR is needed.
