MINIOPOLY v0.3.74 - MOVING PORTALS AND EMBER DAMAGE

Stop hosting and fully Exit Launcher from the Windows system tray.
Apply the update over your complete game folder, or extract Complete into a new
folder. Relaunch, refresh all browsers and confirm v0.3.74.

World Collapse: max 3 active portals per world, relocated every full round.
The existing ramp is 1 per world, then 2, then 3.
Returned properties keep their owner/upgrades. Hot embers cause a random bank
fee on landing: first recovered round $75-$150, second $25-$60, then zero.
Normal rent or the normal purchase decision follows the damage fee.

Active old World Collapse saves receive the cap and new recovery rules.
Spent tokens stay spent; repeat-portal restrictions reset each full round.
Existing construction-point tokens and last-player-standing victory remain.
Countdown and completed results keep their rules.

See EXPRESS-WORLD-COLLAPSE.md, UPDATE-v0.3.74.md and DOWNLOAD-v0.3.74.md.
