MINIOPOLY v0.3.79 - TSUNAMI FLOOD-SHIELD DOMES

Stop hosting and fully Exit Launcher from the Windows system tray.
Apply the update over your complete game folder, or extract Complete into a new
folder. Relaunch, refresh all browsers and confirm v0.3.79.

In Tsunami, properties with unused upgrade protection show a glowing blue dome
with a hexagon pattern. It appears as soon as you upgrade or use a Building
Permit. The first tsunami hit removes one upgrade and spends the protection;
the dome disappears. Re-upgrading does not renew a spent shield.
Existing Tsunami/Countdown rooms, completed records and pending trades resume.

The mode still has 10, 15 or 20 building rounds, then increasing waves of
1, 2, 3... targets. Submerged spaces collect no rent and remain safe to land on.
World Collapse and Standard are unchanged.
Open /tsunami-preview to see sample shield domes and waves.
See DOWNLOAD-v0.3.79.md, UPDATE-v0.3.79.md and TSUNAMI.md.
