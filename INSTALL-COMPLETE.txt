MINIOPOLY v0.3.69 - COUNTDOWN AND MULTIPLAYER TRADING

Stop hosting and fully Exit Launcher from the Windows system tray.
Apply the update over your complete game folder, or extract Complete into a new
folder. Relaunch, refresh every browser and confirm v0.3.69.

Create room > Game Options > Express - Countdown.
Choose 10, 15 or 20 building rounds. Start a NEW match for Countdown.
Trade to complete sets and upgrade properties for one automatic shield each.
Building Permits still grant one free upgrade without a set.
Waves target 1, 2, 3... properties globally, with a full round of notice.
A shield loses one upgrade; a later hit consumes the property.
Losing your last property after a wave eliminates you. Void landings are safe.

Open Trading Center from the visible button above the board or Turn Actions.
Send properties and cash, counter offers, and accept/decline/withdraw.
Offers settle between moves after mandatory payments and purchases.

Existing World Collapse rules remain. Its proposed moving-void revision is
analyzed in WORLD-COLLAPSE-REVISION-PROPOSAL.md for review.
See COUNTDOWN.md, UPDATE-v0.3.69.md and DOWNLOAD-v0.3.69.md.
