MINIOPOLY v0.3.80 - TSUNAMI SEAWALLS

Stop hosting and fully Exit Launcher from the Windows system tray.
Apply the update over your complete game folder, or extract Complete into a new
folder. Relaunch, refresh all browsers and confirm v0.3.80.

Low stone seawalls replace the blue shield bubbles. The building stays visible.
When protection absorbs a tsunami hit, the wall crumbles and disappears.
Re-upgrading does not renew spent protection. Saves and trades can resume.
Tsunami rules, Standard and World Collapse are unchanged.
Open /tsunami-preview for sample seawalls and waves.
See DOWNLOAD-v0.3.80.md, UPDATE-v0.3.80.md and TSUNAMI.md.
