Fully **Exit Game**, extract the Update ZIP into your existing complete game folder and replace files. Restart the host and refresh **every player**. Confirm **v0.3.114**. The update is cumulative; v0.3.110 can update directly. Existing matches and saved Swap Favor cards continue.

Use **Trading Center → Swap Favor** for consensual position swaps. Use **Negotiate rent** on a rent bill to propose or counter a payment. After acceptance, close Trading Center and **Pay** the agreed amount. See [release notes](UPDATE-v0.3.114.md) and download checksums.
