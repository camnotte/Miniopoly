# Miniopoly v0.3.33 — Player portraits and property-set progress

The waiting-room and in-game player cards now use larger portraits in neutral cream frames. The old colored background/dot is removed. Your avatar has a **YOU** badge beneath it. Full names, teal portrait outlines and **YOUR TURN / PLAYING** labels make the active player clear.

Players & Standings displays **Property sets** across each card, above cash and net worth. All eight real sets are included: Brown and Navy have two properties each; the other colors have three. Sets span worlds, matching the actual upgrade rules. A counter such as **1/3** means one of that set's three properties is owned by that player. Completed sets have a checkmark; zero-owned groups are softer. Total deeds also counts railroads and utilities, which are outside the color sets.

Hover over, tap, or keyboard-focus a counter to see every property in that set, its world and owner, availability or bank foreclosure status. Mortgages and upgrade levels appear in the details. A complete mortgaged set is owned in full but still requires lifting mortgages before standard upgrades. Tap to pin details; close with the ×, Escape or a click outside. Details update live and stay below chat.

Money, ranking and set progress retain the existing timing: presentation updates after piece arrival. Scoring, turn order, personal photos, avatar confirmation and moving game pieces are unchanged. Phone players remain collapsed until opened; the expanded list uses one readable card per row.

## Install

1. Stop the game server.
2. Extract **Miniopoly-v0.3.33-Update.zip** and copy all contents into your existing game folder, replacing matching files.
3. Run **Start-Miniopoly.bat**, choose your normal hosting mode and refresh all player browsers. Confirm **v0.3.33** beside Room.

Saved rooms, hosting settings, certificates and all existing launchers are retained.

## Review

Check the larger avatars in the waiting room. Start a match and open Players on a phone if needed. Inspect each player's set progress, then tap a counter and check the worlds, owners and available properties. Buy or sell a property and confirm the counter and details change on both devices. Confirm turn labels follow the active player and the original game pieces still move normally.
