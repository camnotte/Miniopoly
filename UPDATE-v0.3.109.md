# Miniopoly v0.3.109 — Live redevelopment auctions and a board countdown

Blind redevelopment bidding no longer pauses the match. Active players can roll, resolve landings, pay bills, buy and develop properties, trade, rebuild, use shields and end turns while bids are open. Bidding is separate from the current turn phase. Closing or timing out an auction cannot reset a purchase, rent bill, card, escape payment, bonus roll, position or turn.

A clickable countdown strip above the board shows the time left and either Bid now or Bids sealed. It turns amber/red in the final 15 seconds and reopens the current auction. The window can close before submitting; drafts remain when reopened or when another player moves. A shortcut in landing popups also opens bidding or winner results, so a player resolving a landing can still reach them. Results wait for ongoing movement/landing dialogs rather than interrupting a decision. Host timing drives the countdown; refreshing cannot extend a deadline.

Sealed bids remain private and cannot be edited. Their combined amount reserves cash without deducting it or changing net worth. Reserved cash cannot also fund purchases, paid development, rebuilding or accepted trades. Each player sees their own available/reserved cash; another player's bid amounts or reservation totals are not sent. Existing rent/escape/card/tax bills reduce the bidding budget. Required payments take priority: if a bill would need reserved cash, that player's sealed bids become passes, with a notice, before settlement or payment can consume the same money. Only winners pay their winning bids; unused reservations release when bidding ends. Missing submissions still become passes after 90 seconds, and bidding closes earlier when all remaining players submit.

Later waves keep moving even if an auction remains open. Newly destroyed sites queue without duplicating lots already offered. Each queued auction receives its own 90-second deadline when it opens, and queue/deadline/bids survive restart. After the last wave, play continues until outstanding auctions finish. Everyone then gets the next full calm recovery round before final net-worth scoring. If wave five opens no auction and none is pending, its current calm round is the recovery round. A sole survivor still ends the match early and releases outstanding reservations.

Old directional saves paused in the former auction phase resume normal turns after installation without discarding bids or changing the current deadline. Classic, World Collapse and legacy nondirectional Tsunami rules are unchanged. Fully Exit Game, replace files in the complete normal game folder, restart the host, refresh every player and confirm v0.3.109. No match reset is required.
