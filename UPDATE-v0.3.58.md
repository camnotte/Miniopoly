# v0.3.58 — Approved rising championship podium

Stop hosting and choose **Exit Launcher** from the Windows system tray. Extract
the Update ZIP into your **existing v0.3.56 or v0.3.57 game folder**, replacing matching
files. Reopen **Start-Miniopoly.bat**, start Local Multiplayer or Online hosting,
and refresh every player's browser. Confirm **v0.3.58** beside Room. Updating
does not reset rooms; saves, seats, avatars and hosting settings remain in place.
For older installations use the Complete Windows package. Node.js LTS is required.

The approved ceremony is now in the game. The first-place gold column starts
low and grows upward over 4.8 seconds, following a brief pause, while the winner's
portrait rises with it and additional finite confetti falls. Its base stays fixed.
The trophy, selected piece, name and final net worth reveal after the rise.

The large gold cup has red ribbons and a bold, highly visible **1**, without a
star behind it. The piece sits beside the trophy's base with a transparent
background and no frame. **Miniopoly Champion** stays on one line above the cup,
shortening to **Champion** when space is limited. The bold gold title has a slow
3.8-second glow pulse. Nunito remains the font throughout.

Shared winners receive equally tall gold podiums and matching first-place ranks.
Two-player games show two podiums; up to four co-champions fit with responsive
layouts. The final standings retain every player and actual cash, deeds and
upgrade counts. Existing scoring and tie rules are unchanged.

**Skip entrance** settles the podium and clears confetti immediately; **Replay
ceremony** starts fresh. Device/game Reduced Motion shows still results without
fanfare, confetti or title pulse. Leaving the results cancels its visual effects
and sound. Chat remains above the ceremony. **View final board**, **Hall of Fame**,
**Save victory image**, eligible host rematches and each player's independent
**Return to Create / Join** remain available. There is no automatic departure.

To review without ending a match, open
**http://localhost:3000/championship-preview** while hosting locally. Choose
two/three/four players or shared-winner examples. This uses sample results only.
The standalone Championship Preview HTML embeds its art and font.

Community Festival artwork, fireworks, 60/40 awards, all board layouts, the nine
pieces, property rules, saved-game format and working hosting remain unchanged.
