# Rent increase — discussion only

No rent change is implemented in v0.3.82. Tsunami paid upgrades are now 2x the
standard price; Building Permits are still free and upgrades still give one use
of flood protection. Rent helps fund trades and upgrades, while the tsunami
continues to remove earning properties.

| Option | A current $12 rent becomes | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep current rent | $12 | Gentler cash pressure, but paid upgrades take longer to earn back their cost. |
| +25% once tsunami waves begin | $15 | Recommended first trial: surviving properties earn more without a large cash drain during building. |
| +50% once waves begin | $18 | Stronger reward and pressure; more risk of players lacking cash for upgrades/trades. |
| Double rent | $24 | A harsh combination with doubled upgrade costs; a few expensive landings may dominate the property-survival ending. |

Recommendation: start with +25% on active, surviving properties in Tsunami from
the first wave. Keep building rounds, Standard and World Collapse unchanged.
Submerged, mortgaged or rent-suspended properties must still charge no rent.
Round fractional boosted rents upward, consistent with the existing optional
World Collapse bonus. Decide after testing whether +50% is needed instead.

The existing optional World Collapse Final Showdown remains its separate +50%
rule; this proposal does not enable or alter it.
