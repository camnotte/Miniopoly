# Miniopoly v0.3.31 — Avatar rows and personal photo frame

Create Room and Join Room now have separate scrollable rows for the five men and five women. Each row has its own left/right arrows and phone swipe support. Your selection stays the same while browsing.

An oval personal-photo frame sits beside the form heading, separate from the cartoon portraits. The host introduction breaks after “once” to leave room beside it. The old Upload photo / Remove photo button row is replaced by the frame and a small trash icon near its bottom.

- Click the empty frame to upload a photo.
- Click a cartoon portrait to select it while keeping your photo available.
- Click a saved, unselected photo to use it again.
- Click an already selected photo to replace it.
- Click the trash icon to clear the saved photo and return to a cartoon avatar.

Photo formats, resizing, room synchronization and saved preferences retain the v0.3.30 behavior. Moving game pieces and their effects are unchanged. Existing room seats keep their joined avatar; choose a different avatar when creating or joining a new room.

## Install

Stop the game server. Extract **Miniopoly-v0.3.31-Update.zip** and copy all contents into your existing game folder, replacing matching files. Run **Start-Miniopoly.bat**, choose your usual hosting mode and refresh all players' browsers. Confirm **v0.3.31** beside Room.

All existing launchers are included. Saved rooms, hosting settings and certificates remain in place.

## Review

Check that Men and Women scroll independently. Click the oval beside Host a new game to upload a photo, select a cartoon, then click the saved photo to select it again. Check that the trash icon removes the photo. Repeat on Join Room and a phone. Start with one personal avatar and one cartoon avatar and confirm both devices show the same portraits after refreshing.
